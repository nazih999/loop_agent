## Build a Mobile Floating Clipboard App

### Concept:
A mobile version of FloatBoard that works as a floating bubble (like Messenger Chat Heads) that stays on top of other apps. Users can paste text, images, and links there, and access them instantly without switching apps.

---

## Core Features:

### 1. Floating Bubble (Always on Top)
- A small bubble that floats over all apps
- Draggable anywhere on screen
- Tap to open/close the clipboard panel
- Works system-wide (even outside the app)

### 2. Clipboard History
- Automatically saves everything copied (text, links, images)
- Shows last 50 items
- Tap any item to copy it again
- Search through clipboard history
- Pin important items to top

### 3. Quick Notes
- Write quick notes inside the bubble panel
- Auto-save
- Pin notes to bubble
- Swipe to delete

### 4. Screenshot Markup
- After taking a screenshot, open it instantly in the app
- Draw arrows, circles, rectangles
- Add text on top
- Crop and share directly

### 5. Smart Organization
- Auto-categorize clips: Links, Emails, Phone Numbers, Addresses
- Color-code by category
- Star/favorite important clips

### 6. Cloud Sync (Optional Premium)
- Sync clipboard across devices
- Backup to Google Drive
- Restore on new phone

---

## User Stories (9 Scenarios):

### Story 1: The Student
Fatima is studying on her phone. She copies key points from a PDF and they auto-save to FloatBoard. She opens the bubble, reviews her notes, and copies them to her study group on WhatsApp.

### Story 2: The Developer
Yazan finds a useful code snippet on Twitter. He copies it. FloatBoard saves it. Later, he opens the bubble while coding on his laptop, and the snippet is there.

### Story 3: The Designer
Omar finds 5 inspiration images on Instagram. He copies them one by one. FloatBoard keeps them all. He opens the bubble to compare them side by side.

### Story 4: The Writer
Sarah is writing an article. She copies research notes, quotes, and references. FloatBoard categorizes them: Links, Quotes, Notes. She accesses them without leaving her writing app.

### Story 5: The Business Owner
Ahmed receives a client's address and phone number on WhatsApp. He copies them. FloatBoard auto-detects: "Phone: 06XX" and "Address: 123 Street". He taps to call or open Maps.

### Story 6: The Forgetful User
Omar copied a discount code yesterday. Today he needs it. He opens FloatBoard, searches "discount", and finds it instantly.

### Story 7: The Screenshot User
Fatima takes a screenshot of a chart. FloatBoard opens it instantly. She draws an arrow pointing to a key number, adds text "Important", and sends it to her boss.

### Story 8: The Multi-Device User
Yazan copies a link on his phone. FloatBoard syncs it. He opens his tablet, and the link is there in the bubble.

### Story 9: The Minimalist
Sarah wants zero clutter. She only uses the floating bubble. One tap = her clipboard. No need to open a full app. No ads. No complexity.

---

## Technical Requirements:
- Platform: Android (Kotlin/Java)
- Permissions: SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, READ_CLIPBOARD
- Architecture: Floating Service, Clipboard Manager, Local Database (Room)
